## Context

An ArchiMate picture shows four kinds of box. A domain box and a type box are computed by the renderer; a type box is named by a counter and has no stable identity. An authored grouping is diagram content, identified only by its label and position in a list. A container element is an element drawn with others inside it because the model holds a composition or aggregation. An author who may size and move one kind of box expects to size, move, create and delete the others.

Alternatives considered:

- **Leave computed boxes fixed.** Half the boxes on a picture could not be arranged.
- **A third structure for computed boxes**, beside authored groupings and layout. Three concepts for one thing a reader sees.
- **One grouping concept whose membership is either a list or a rule** (chosen).

## Decision

A grouping holds either the members it lists or a rule that selects them: a domain, or an element type within its parent. Groupings of either kind nest. A listed grouping claims its members first, then the most specific rule.

A diagram that states no ruled grouping draws the automatic boxes it draws today, unchanged. The first time one is created or deleted in the editor, the current automatic boxes are written down as ruled groupings, and returning to automatic boxes removes them again.

Every grouping has a stable identity of its own. A box not yet written down is addressed by its rule, never by a renderer's counter. A statement about one drawn relation, such as its label or its route, is keyed by the connection it draws and, where an element is drawn more than once, by the instances at its ends.

A box is as large as its contents need and may be stated larger, never smaller. Deleting a grouping moves its members to its parent.

## Consequences

- Existing diagrams change format: groupings gain identities and per-relation statements gain the wider key. The [Repository Upgrade Framework](../../../projects/platform-core/model/application/application-component/APP@1783872532.OZbaBh.repository-upgrade-framework.md) assigns them, and only for the tiers a deployment owns.
- The body generator honours an explicit list of groupings whether or not the diagram has a layout.
- A ruled grouping is a drop target only for elements its rule admits.
- Not yet implemented.
