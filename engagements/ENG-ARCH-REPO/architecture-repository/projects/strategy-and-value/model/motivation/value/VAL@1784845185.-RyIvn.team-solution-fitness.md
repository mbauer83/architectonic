## Team Solution Fitness

The standing worth, to delivery stakeholders, of a team's work actually solving its domain-specific problem to spec — effectiveness at the local scope. This is what local autonomy is for; uncoordinated, it can rise locally while eroding enterprise solution fitness (a local optimum diverging from the global optimum). Undated and interest-relative; sustained divergence eventually threatens the team's own standing.
