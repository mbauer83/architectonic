## Enterprise Adaptability

The standing worth, to leadership, architects, and product stakeholders, of the enterprise being able to change and re-shape itself as a whole in response to its environment — including the customer-facing facet, where the complete product (implemented across several teams) and the services the enterprise offers respond to customer and market learning, alongside internal reconfiguration of teams, services, and processes. This is the enterprise's adaptive capacity over time; it becomes coherent only when integration (unity of effort) holds. Its importance rises with the demand for faster product and service learning cycles.
