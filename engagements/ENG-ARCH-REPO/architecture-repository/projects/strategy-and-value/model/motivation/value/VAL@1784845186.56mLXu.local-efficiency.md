## Local Efficiency

The standing worth, to teams, of getting their work done with proportionate effort. Modeled separately from team solution fitness because integration and architecture work taxes it in the short term while protecting it over the medium and long term. Its short-term cost is what the reduce-overhead, faster-planning, and agent-native goals exist to minimise. Undated and interest-relative; unmanaged short-term cost can erode a team's standing.
