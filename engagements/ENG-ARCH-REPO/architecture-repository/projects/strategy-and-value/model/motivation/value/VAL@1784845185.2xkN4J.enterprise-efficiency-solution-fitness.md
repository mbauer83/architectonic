## Enterprise Efficiency & Solution Fitness

The standing worth, to Enterprise Leadership, of the enterprise both delivering offerings that fit real customer and market demand (solution fitness — effectiveness at enterprise scale) and doing so with proportionate resource (efficiency). A deliberately two-headed value: at this scope the two connect identically — both valued by leadership, both eroded by uncoordinated local optima, both served by unity of effort — so they are combined under the shared role. Undated and interest-relative.
