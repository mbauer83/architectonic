## Local Autonomy

The standing worth of holding decision authority at every level it recurs — business unit, team, and developer-within-a-team — over processes, standards, formats, communication structures, technical and organizational strategies, services, and internal or outward-facing solutions, without unnecessary dependence on central coordination. The local adaptability this freedom buys — the ability to change locally in response to a team's own situation — is part of this worth, not a separate value. Undated and interest-relative; sustained erosion of it eventually threatens a team's own viability.
