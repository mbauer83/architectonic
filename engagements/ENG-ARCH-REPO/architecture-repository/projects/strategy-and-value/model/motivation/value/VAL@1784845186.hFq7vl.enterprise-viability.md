## Enterprise Viability

The standing worth of the enterprise's continued capacity to survive and thrive, resting on its global — not merely local — effectiveness, efficiency, and adaptability: locally effective, efficient, adaptable parts do not automatically compose into an effective, efficient, adaptable whole. Contributed to by Enterprise Adaptability and Enterprise Efficiency & Solution Fitness; it is the target the medium- and long-term benefit of integration ultimately protects — the reason to give up a little short-term efficiency to stay viable. Undated and interest-relative.
