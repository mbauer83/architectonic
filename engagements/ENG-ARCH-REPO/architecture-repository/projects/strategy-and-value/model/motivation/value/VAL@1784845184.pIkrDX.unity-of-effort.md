## Unity of Effort

The standing worth, to Enterprise Leadership and other coordinating stakeholders, of differentiated, partially autonomous contributors remaining sufficiently coordinated, mutually intelligible, and aligned that their combined effects keep serving the purposes and viability of the enterprise as a whole. Global coherence is constitutive of it: concepts used by different parts of the enterprise stay mutually intelligible, dependencies stay sufficiently understood, local decisions stay compatible with cross-cutting constraints, and work stays traceable to the motivations and requirements that justify it. Interest-relative and undated; distinct from the enterprise's adaptive capacity over time, which it makes coherent.
