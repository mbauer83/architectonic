## Assurance Store Confinement Test Suite

`tests/assurance/test_store_confinement_pragmas.py` — reads the store's confinement pragmas back off a live connection and asserts that the store requested them rather than inheriting them from the SQLCipher build. Covers `temp_store = MEMORY` (SQLite writes a spilled temp b-tree outside SQLCipher's encryption boundary), `secure_delete = ON`, that both hold on a second thread's connection and across a lock/unlock cycle, and that a store created before the pragmas existed gains them on its next open. Run by the backend quality gate on every change.
