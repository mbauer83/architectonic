## Proposed Change

One edit an engagement suggests to an enterprise artifact it may not write: which artifact, what it should say, what the artifact was when the suggestion was written, and how far the suggestion has got.

## Notes

The counterpart of promotion. Promotion moves content an engagement owns up to the enterprise tier; this carries a change to content the enterprise already owns, which an engagement may read and not write.

**It records the edit, not a patch.** What is kept is the authoring call the proposer would have made, so it can be applied later to whatever the artifact has become. A textual patch would be positional against a target that moves, and a rendered diff would compare against a rendering rather than against the content.

**It records what the artifact was.** Without that, a suggestion that has already been applied upstream cannot be told from one that never was, and a reviewer is asked to resolve their own accepted work.

Held until it is settled, whether it was accepted or withdrawn: what was suggested and what became of it is the audit a reviewer needs later, and deleting it leaves only the enterprise history, which records what was applied and never what was asked.
