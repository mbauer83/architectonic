## Enterprise Content Moved Under a Pending Change

The enterprise artifact a pending proposed change was written against is no longer what it was: a reviewer merged something, or another engagement promoted. Nothing announces it; the engagement finds out when it next reads the enterprise tier.

## Notes

From here on a pending change can no longer be assumed to apply. Whether the movement accepted the change, left it applicable or made it impossible is not part of the event; the behaviour it triggers decides that, one change at a time.
