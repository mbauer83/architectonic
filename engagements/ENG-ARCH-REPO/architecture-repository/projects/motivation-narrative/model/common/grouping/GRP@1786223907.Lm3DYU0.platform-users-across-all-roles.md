## Platform Users Across All Roles

Every stakeholder who uses the platform, gathered as one. Some concerns are not held by a particular role but by all of them — the ability to record a thought before it has a type is wanted by an architect, a product owner and a compliance officer alike. Naming a single role as the stakeholder for such a concern would be false; aggregating them says the true thing, which is that the concern is general. Aggregation rather than composition, because each stakeholder stands on its own and belongs to other concerns besides this one.
