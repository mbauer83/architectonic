## Enable Concurrent, Staged Collaborative Planning

Let many teams and agents plan concurrently on a shared model, staging local drafts through explicit promotion toward a governed shared tier, so collaborative planning scales without a central editing bottleneck. Concurrent local work and cross-stakeholder validation proceed together rather than diverging into isolated, unreconciled contexts.

| Measurability | All stakeholder groups can navigate the model to address their primary concerns |
