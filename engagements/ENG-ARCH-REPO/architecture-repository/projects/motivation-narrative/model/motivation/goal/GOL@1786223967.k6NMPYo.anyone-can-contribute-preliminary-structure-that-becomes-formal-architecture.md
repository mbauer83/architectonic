## Anyone Can Contribute Preliminary Structure That Becomes Formal Architecture

The intent: that a contributor who has not settled on a structure — and who may not know the meta-ontology at all — can record what they are thinking in the platform, and that what they record can progress into verified model content without being re-entered. Both halves matter. Contribution without progression is a whiteboard; progression without low-barrier contribution is what the platform already had.
