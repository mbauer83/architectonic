## Sustain Unity of Effort at Agentic Velocity

Sustain unity of effort — autonomous contributors taking different local actions while those actions stay compatible with shared enterprise intent — even as local autonomy increases and change moves at agentic velocity. Unity of effort is sustained *while local autonomy increases*, not by curtailing it: by making shared intent, interfaces, constraints, dependencies, decisions, and assurance obligations explicit and machine-checkable rather than by routing routine decisions through central approval.

## Properties

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Measurability | Reduction in cross-team integration failures, governance rework rate, and measured coherence of plans and built artefacts across concurrent agentic SDLC work |
| Priority | Must |
