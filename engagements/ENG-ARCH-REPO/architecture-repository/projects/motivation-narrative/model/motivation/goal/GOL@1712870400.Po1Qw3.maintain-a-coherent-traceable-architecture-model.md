## Maintain a Coherent, Traceable Architecture Model

Keep the architecture model a faithful, referentially sound, and traceable representation — entities, connections, diagrams, and documents consistent, indexable, searchable, and traceable, with realization chains intact as diverse human and AI authors edit together — *so that* what the enterprise plans, builds, and operates can stay coherent and accountable. Model fidelity is a means to coherent enterprise action, never an end in itself.

## Properties

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Measurability | Zero referential integrity errors in verified model; complete realization chain from motivation to technology |
| Priority | Must |
