## Enable Coherent, Traceable Enterprise Action

The desired state in which what the enterprise decides, builds, operates, and presents stays coherent, traceable, and mutually compatible — most critically where independently-evolving products, services, processes, standards, formats, strategies, shared concepts, communication structures, and solutions meet across team, solution, engagement, or enterprise boundaries. This is the end that model fidelity serves: maintaining a coherent, traceable architecture model is the subordinate means that positively influences this goal. Distinct from model fidelity — this concerns the real-world coherence and traceability of enterprise action itself.
