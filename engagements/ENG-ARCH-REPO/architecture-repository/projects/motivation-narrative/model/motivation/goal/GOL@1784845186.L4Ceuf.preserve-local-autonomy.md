## Preserve Local Autonomy

The desired state of keeping decision authority with teams and agents — across processes, standards, formats, communication structures, strategies, services, and solutions — so they can adapt locally to their own situation. Autonomy is bounded only where impact and risk warrant it; that bounding is carried by the subordinate outcomes, the proportionality principle, and the opposition to fragmentation, not by curtailing the intent named here.
