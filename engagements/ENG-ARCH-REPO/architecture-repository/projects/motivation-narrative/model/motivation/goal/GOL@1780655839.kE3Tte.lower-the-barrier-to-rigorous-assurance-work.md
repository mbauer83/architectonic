## Lower the Barrier to Rigorous Assurance Work

Reduce the method, legal, and setup friction of assurance work and actively support and accelerate it, so it keeps pace with agentic development and experts spend less effort per analysis without diminishing their role. Scalable, guided assurance keeps specialist availability from becoming a central coordination bottleneck.

## Properties

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Measurability | Measurable reduction in time to complete a first assurance analysis; method coaching and guidance available without external specialist dependency; assurance throughput does not become the bottleneck for agentic development |
| Priority | Must |
