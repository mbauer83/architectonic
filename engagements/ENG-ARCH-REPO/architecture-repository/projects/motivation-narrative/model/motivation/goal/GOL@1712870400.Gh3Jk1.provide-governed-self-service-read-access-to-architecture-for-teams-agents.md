## Provide Governed Self-Service Read Access to Architecture for Teams & Agents

Give stakeholders and implementing teams governed, self-service read access to architectural work, including programmatic access for agents via MCP or RAG, so they can retrieve relevant architecture on demand without depending on a specialist or a central gatekeeper.
