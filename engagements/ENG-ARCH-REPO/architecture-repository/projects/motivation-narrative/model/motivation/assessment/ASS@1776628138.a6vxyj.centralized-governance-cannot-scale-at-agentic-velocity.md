## Centralized Governance Cannot Scale at Agentic Velocity

At agentic volume and concurrency, routing routine decisions through centralized architecture review boards and approval mechanisms turns the coordination step into a throughput bottleneck. Preserving throughput then forces a choice between suppressing local autonomy and responsiveness or weakening review — and neither sustains enterprise coherence. Tool-enforced governance and distributed standards become essential.
