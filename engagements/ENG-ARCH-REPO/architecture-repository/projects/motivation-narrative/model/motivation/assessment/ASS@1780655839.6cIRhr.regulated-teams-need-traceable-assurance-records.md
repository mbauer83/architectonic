## Regulated Teams Need Traceable Assurance Records

Teams subject to applicable regulations need assurance records that are traceable end-to-end — from obligation through analysis, control, and evidence to the architecture they concern — so auditors and regulators can follow the chain. Tamper-evident, retained storage is one implementation means of protecting those records, not the fundamental motivational need.
