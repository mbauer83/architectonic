## Alternatives and Reasoning Are Discarded Before They Reach the Repository

What arrives in the repository is the answer, not the answers that were considered and set aside, nor why. The rejected options and the reasoning that discriminated between them are lost at the boundary, so nothing can later explain why a capability exists in this shape rather than another. This is a secondary consequence of the same exclusion, and a real one: the record is thinner than the thinking that produced it.
