## Autonomy at Agentic Velocity Threatens Unity of Effort

At agentic velocity, autonomous teams and agents can optimize locally across their processes, standards, formats, communication structures, strategies, services, and solutions while producing incompatible decisions and interfaces across boundaries. Autonomy is not the problem — unintegrated cross-boundary effects are. This can fragment products and customer-facing behaviour, but it can equally break integration among internal or externally consumed services without changing product features. The resulting local optima erode shared intent and the adaptability of the enterprise as a whole unless integration is deliberately and efficiently maintained.
