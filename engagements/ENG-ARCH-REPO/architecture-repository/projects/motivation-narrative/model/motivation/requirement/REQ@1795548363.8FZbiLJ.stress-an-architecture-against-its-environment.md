## Stress an Architecture Against Its Environment

The system shall let an architect record the stressors an architecture may meet, the end states they lead to and the adaptations that answer them, with each adaptation held as an alternative to the architecture under analysis, so that which stressors the adapted architecture survives can be judged against the model.
