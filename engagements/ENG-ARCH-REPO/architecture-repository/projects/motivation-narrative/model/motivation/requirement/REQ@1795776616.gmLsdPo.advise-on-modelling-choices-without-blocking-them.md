## Advise on Modelling Choices Without Blocking Them

The system shall report a modelling heuristic that a model departs from as advice, a finding kind distinct from errors and warnings that never refuses a write, a save or a promotion. An author can acknowledge an advice on the element it concerns, with a reason, after which it stays quiet; errors and warnings cannot be acknowledged this way.
