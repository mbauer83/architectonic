## Views State Their Stakeholders and Concerns

The system shall let a viewpoint and a diagram state who the view is for and which concerns it answers, each entry either prose or a reference to a stakeholder or motivation element of the model, and shall refuse a reference that does not resolve to an element of an admitted type. A diagram that applies a viewpoint shall carry the viewpoint's statement wherever it states none of its own.
