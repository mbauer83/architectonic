## Promotion Mechanism to Enterprise Repository

The system shall provide a tool-mediated mechanism to promote individual artifacts from an engagement repository to the enterprise repository. Promotion shall automatically include the transitive closure of the artifact's dependency graph (all entities and connections it depends on). Promotion is staged and reviewable before it takes effect: the selection is validated, conflicts against the enterprise repository are detected and resolved, and the process validates ID uniqueness within the enterprise repository and ensures that promoted artifacts meet enterprise-level quality criteria (status, completeness, referential integrity). No artifact reaches the enterprise tier without passing that staged review.
