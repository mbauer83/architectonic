## Hold Untyped Scratchpad Notes and Links on a Spatial Canvas

The platform must accept a note carrying nothing but a title, and a link carrying nothing but two endpoints, arranged spatially on a canvas. No type may be demanded at creation, and no verification may run before there is something to verify.
