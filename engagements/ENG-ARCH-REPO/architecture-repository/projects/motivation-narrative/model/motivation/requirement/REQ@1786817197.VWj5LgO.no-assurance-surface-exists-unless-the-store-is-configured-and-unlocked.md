## No Assurance Surface Exists Unless the Store Is Configured and Unlocked

The confidential assurance capability is gated fail-closed rather than permission-checked: the assurance MCP servers and the GUI's assurance features do not exist as reachable surfaces unless a store is both configured and unlocked. In a deployment that has not configured one, no assurance content is reachable by any route, and the absence is structural — there is no surface to authorize against. Unlocking authorizes the running backend; locking revokes access and closes the store while the key stays in the keychain.
