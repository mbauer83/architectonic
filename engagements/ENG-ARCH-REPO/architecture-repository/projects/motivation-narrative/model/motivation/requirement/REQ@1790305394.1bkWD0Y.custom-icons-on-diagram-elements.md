## Custom Icons on Diagram Elements

A diagram's elements can show a picture chosen for them — a product logo or one of the organisation's own — beside the element's name or as the element itself, without changing the model. A picture is stated for one element, for every element of a type or specialization on the diagram, or as a specialization's default, and the most specific statement wins. A picture is uploaded once, checked and reduced to one stored form, and carries whose mark it is and what it may be shown on; an upload that cannot be made safe to draw is refused. Every diagram body carries the pictures it draws, so it renders wherever PlantUML does, and a promotion takes a diagram's pictures with it without the enterprise library holding one picture twice.
