## Report Decidable Defects in a Rendered Diagram

The system shall report, as findings on the diagram, what the geometry of its rendered picture decides without a person looking: a relation drawn through an element it does not connect, shapes that overlap, a label wider than its shape, and a box drawn over an element that is not its member. Relation crossings and detours are counted to direct a reader's attention and are never findings, since a crossing is often unavoidable. A picture that cannot be read is reported as such; its geometry is not guessed.
