## Hold Planning Artifacts and Relate Them to the Architecture They Change

The system shall hold the artifacts a planned change is described in — and relate each to the architecture elements it changes — so that planning and architecture are one addressable body rather than two, and a reader can move from a piece of planning to what it touches and back. What the planning content is made of is not yet designed: the document-types and diagram-types it needs, and how they reference architecture elements, remain open. Judging whether planning and architecture agree is work an agent or reviewer does over prose; the obligation here is only that both sides are held and related closely enough for that judgement to be possible without reconstructing either.
