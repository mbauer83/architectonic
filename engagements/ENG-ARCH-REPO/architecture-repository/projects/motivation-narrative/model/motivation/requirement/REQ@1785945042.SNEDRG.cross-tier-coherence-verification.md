## Cross-Tier Coherence Verification

The system shall verify that a change preserves coherence across the engagement and enterprise tiers before it is committed — checking referential integrity over the combined-scope read rather than over either tier alone, and verifying the staged repository as a whole, so a write that would leave a dangling or contradictory cross-tier reference is refused rather than reported after the fact.
