## Verify a Scratchpad Link Against the Meta-Ontology When Both Ends Are Typed

The platform must check a drawn link the moment both of its endpoints carry an element type, refusing what the ontology forbids and naming the permitted alternatives — including the reverse direction where that is what is permitted. Before both ends are typed it must say nothing.
