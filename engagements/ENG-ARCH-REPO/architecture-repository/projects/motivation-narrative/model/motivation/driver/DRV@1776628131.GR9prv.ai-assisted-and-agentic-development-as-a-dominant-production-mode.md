## AI-Assisted and Agentic Development as a Dominant Production Mode

The adoption of AI-assisted and agentic development as a primary mode of software production is a rapidly accelerating industry-wide trend, raising the volume, concurrency, and velocity of change across code, configuration, tests, documentation, infrastructure, and architecture-relevant decisions, shifting the economics of software production, and creating new demands on how architectural knowledge must be structured, accessed, and governed. Industry practice is converging on structured, persisted, queryable specifications as the working context for these agents.
