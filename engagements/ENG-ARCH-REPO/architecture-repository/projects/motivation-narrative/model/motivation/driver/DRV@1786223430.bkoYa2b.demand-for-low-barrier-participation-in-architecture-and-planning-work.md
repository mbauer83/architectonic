## Demand for Low-Barrier Participation in Architecture and Planning Work

The people whose knowledge the model needs — product, delivery, operations, domain experts — are largely not fluent in a meta-ontology, and the demand for their participation is rising as architecture work moves closer to delivery. A practice that admits only those who can name a type before they can record a thought confines itself to a narrow group and loses the rest.
