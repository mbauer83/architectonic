## Governance Is Proportional to Impact and Risk

Governance and assurance effort scales with the impact and risk of a decision, wherever they arise. High impact confined to a single team's own solutions still warrants more governance; a boundary crossing is one source of impact, not the criterion itself. Low-impact, low-risk work should not pay the coordination cost of consequential change. Qualified by Safety Is Never Subordinate to Risk: proportionality may reduce unnecessary effort but cannot waive mandatory safety constraints.
