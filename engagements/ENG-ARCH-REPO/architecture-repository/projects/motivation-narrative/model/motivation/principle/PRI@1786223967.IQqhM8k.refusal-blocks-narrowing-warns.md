## Refusal Blocks; Narrowing Warns

Where the ontology forbids a construction the platform refuses it and says so plainly; where a construction is permitted but falls outside what a specialization or a configured scope intends, the platform proceeds and warns. Conflating the two is what makes a tool feel either unsafe or obstructive: a warning that blocks teaches users to route around the tool, and a refusal that only warns lets invalid content into the model.
