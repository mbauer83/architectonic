## Enterprise Leadership

The stakeholder perspective responsible for enterprise-wide direction, portfolio coherence, product and service integration, allocation of decision rights, and the adaptability and survivability of the organization as a whole. Distinct from Upper Technical Management, which is concerned with the technical estate and engineering system; Enterprise Leadership owns the broader organizational and portfolio-level trade-off between local autonomy and enterprise-wide optimization.
