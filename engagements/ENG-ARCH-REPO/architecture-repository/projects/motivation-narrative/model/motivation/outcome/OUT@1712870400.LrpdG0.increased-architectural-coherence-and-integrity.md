## Increased Architectural Coherence and Integrity

A measurable outcome of deploying the architecture repository: architectural artifacts across the organization become more consistent, traceable, and internally coherent. Measured by: reduction in referential integrity errors, increase in cross-domain connection coverage, decrease in orphaned entities (entities without connections), and stakeholder-reported improvement in architectural clarity. This outcome is realized when the goals of coherence, traceability, and cross-stakeholder validation are achieved. This coherence is what lets architects and reviewers validate that what is planned and built serves the stated goals, and locate conflicts between goals, requirements and designs early — value that does not depend on how code production evolves.

## Properties

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Metric | Referential integrity error count, connection coverage ratio, orphaned entity count |
| Target | Zero integrity errors; >80% of entities connected to at least one other entity |
