## Scratchpad Area

A region of a scratchpad that scopes the subject matter its notes describe and, by carrying a permitted set of types, determines which refinements are possible within it. Most areas permit several domains, which is why narrowing to a domain is a real first step rather than a formality. Not a locus of work: an area describes things — structure, behaviour, thoughts, decisions — and the only behaviour occurring in it is the adding and refining of its notes.
